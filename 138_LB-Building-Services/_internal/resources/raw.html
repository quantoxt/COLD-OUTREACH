Welcome to LB Building Services

Domestic Building Services

Domestic work is our bread and butter here at LB Building Services and our passion is working with our clients to help maintain and improve their homes. We offer a wide range of services and no job is too big or small so please contact us and we would be happy to discuss your requirements.

Commercial Building Services

We also have experience in site management and commercial construction and undertake a wide range of commercial building works for national contractors, councils, and businesses.
Recent projects include a city centre hotel and several convenience store extensions.
Please contact us if you would like any more information or would like to discuss a project

Project Management

At LB Building Services, we can also manage your project from the start to the end. We act as the main contractor, taking away the headache of budgeting, purchasing of materials, scheduling and integration of different trades. We ensure the logistics and day to day running of your project run smoothly and we always ensure your site is left clean and tidy at the end of the day! Please contact us if you have a project you would like to discuss in more detail.
