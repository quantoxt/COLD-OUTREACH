LPS Services

LPS of Guiseley, Leeds, is a family-owned business that provides heating, plumbing, electrical services and home fix services for family homes and commercial premises.

Worcester Bosch Diamond Accredited Installers – Gas Safe, OFTEC and  NAPIT Registered Engineers.

We have over 25 years of experience in installing, servicing, and repairing all makes of gas and oil boilers in the Leeds, Bradford, Skipton, and Harrogate areas.

    Heating & Plumbing Services

    Our team delivers professional plumbing, heating, and air conditioning services for homes and commercial properties.

    From repairing leaks and installing boilers to fitting energy-efficient underfloor heating, heat pumps, and climate control systems, we handle it all.
    View heating & plumbing 

    Electrical Services

    We specialise in providing expert electrical services for homes and commercial premises, covering installations, repairs, and maintenance.

    Our services include everything from wiring and system upgrades to socket changes and energy-efficient light installations.

    Home Services

    Our Home Fix team provides reliable home improvement and repair services to help keep your property looking its best.

    From bathroom upgrades and flooring refreshes to lighting improvements and sealant replacement, we take care of the jobs that make a real difference to everyday living.
