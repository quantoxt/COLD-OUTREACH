Gecko Interiors your friendly painting and decorating service Liverpool

Gecko Interiors is a professional, friendly, female painting and decorating service based in Liverpool and covering the North West. Our business has been built around the core values of honesty, integrity, professionalism and reliability. We believe in working with our customers to ensure that their expectations are not just met, but exceeded. We will visit your home to discuss your ideas and requirements and from that initial consultation we will provide you with a full quote detailing all of the work that will be carried out, so that you can be confident there will be no hidden extras. We are happy to offer advice and suggestions to ensure that you maximise the potential of your home.

Having any tradespeople working in your home can be a stressful experience and at Gecko Interiors we understand that good, honest and clear communication is vital for all of our customers as it gives both reassurance and peace of mind. We pride ourselves on being reliable and trustworthy and completing work to the deadlines agreed. We are always aware that we are in your home and endeavour to be as tidy and respectful of your property as possible. Apart from a great finished result, you won’t know we’ve been there!

Gecko Interiors cover all aspects of painting and decorating ranging from single rooms to whole house projects. It’s not just rooms we decorate, we can also give the outside of your home a fresh new look and offer full exterior decorating services.

Alongside our residential services we also offer a comprehensive range of commercial decorating services. From general maintenance painting and insurance work to bespoke office and shop designs, we cover it all.  Please see our commercial section for more information on the types of services we provide.

At Gecko Interiors we understand that every project we undertake reflects an investment from our customers and we strive to ensure that every customer gets real value for money. If you’re looking for a professional painting and decorating service in Liverpool and the North West, give Gecko a call, you won’t be disappointed!
