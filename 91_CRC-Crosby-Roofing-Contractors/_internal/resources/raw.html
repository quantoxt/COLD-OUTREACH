About Us

Your Liverpool Based Roofing Specialists

Crosby Roofing Contractors (CRC) Are your local and reliable roofing specialists. CRC have been installing, repairing and maintaining roofs for over 30 Years, covering the whole of Liverpool & Crosby. CRC also offer commercial roofing services for businesses around Liverpool. Some of our commercial clients include Merchant Taylors School, The Northern Cricket Club, Bullen Health Care Medical Suppliers and Lady Green Garden Centre.

CRC are tremendously proud of the reputation they have built up around Liverpool for over 3 decades. Much of CRC’s work comes via word of mouth as clients regularly recommend them to friends and family. With a dedicated team of skilled roofers Crosby Roofing Contractors strive to offer a 5-star service for an affordable price. With all workmanship guaranteed and finance options available, CRC are then safe pair of hands you have been looking for to complete your next roofing project.
