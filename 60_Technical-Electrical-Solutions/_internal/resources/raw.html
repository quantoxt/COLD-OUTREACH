Our story is our pride

We are Technical Electrical Solutions, TES is a family run business, established in 2005 and is owned & directed by David Nestor. With over 15 years experience in the electrical industry, our team of registered NICEIC electricians in London & Birmingham work closely together to ensure effective communication, efficiency, competency and customer satisfaction always come first.

We have Pimlico electricians, South London Electricians and electricians in London who cover anywhere within the M25. Our electricians in Birmingham  cover the whole of Birmingham giving customers  complete assurance that no matter where they are in London or Birmingham, all electrical works are carried out in accordance with British Standards. Our engineers are on call 24 hours, 365 days of the year, ready to attend and carry out an excellent service to our customers. Our friendly, competent, fully qualified engineers have all undergone vigorous training to keep them up to date with the constant changes in the regulations and legislation. Our electrical engineers are also screened and DBS checked to make sure the general public and our customers are in safe hands at all times

Our Key features

  • A family run company with over 15 years of experience
  • All work overseen by the founder & owner of TES
  • All our engineers are fully qualified & work to British Standard BS7671
  • Registered NICEIC electricians in London & Birmingham
  • Free quotations prior to any works commencing
  • Fairly, reasonably & competitively priced
  • We have Pimlico Electricians, Electrician Camberwell, emergency electrician West London, Electricians in  South London, North, East & West London & Birmingham electricians
  • We offer a one-hour response time when required, available 24 hours  365 days of the year.
