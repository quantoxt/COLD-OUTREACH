McCluskey Joiners is a well-established company with 35 years' experience of providing high-quality carpentry and construction solutions.

We specialise in all aspects of joinery and building, from simple lock repairs to loft conversions and garage construction.

Do you need a craftsman who you can trust to offer the best quality workmanship for either a long- or short-term project? McCluskey Joiners operates Scotland-wide, and offers a prompt and reliable service whatever the job. We operate on commercial and residential properties and regard no job as too small.

McCluskey Joiners have no call out charges and estimates are free. We are trusted by all our clients from elderly single people to large companies requiring full-office refurbishment contracts.

We are qualified, experienced and use the right tools for the job. We can arrange subcontracting for electrical, plumbing, plastering and decorating work and can project-manage the entire workflow, from estimate to completion.
