R & D Loft Ladders are a family run business covering Glasgow, Edinburgh and Central Scotland.

R&D Loft Ladders are a business run by David & Yvonne McKay, working with our family team of David & Marc.
We cover Glasgow, East Dunbartonshire, North Lanarkshire, South Lanarkshire, East Renfrewshire, Renfrewshire, Inverclyde, Falkirk, Stirling etc. and most of the Edinburgh area.

We are a reliable, courteous and fully insured company ready to carry out your loft ladder requirements.
We are dedicated to providing quick, clean & professional loft ladder installations, flooring and lighting in your area, using high quality fully guaranteed materials by leading manufacturers.
