Tamsin Kelly Gardens

Transforming gardens in Stoke Newington, Hackney, Islington, Hampstead, Highgate and throughout North London and North East London

Garden design

Specialising in border design and planting, my work includes contemporary and traditional planting schemes for front and back gardens, gravel gardens, roof gardens, small courtyards and larger family gardens.

Garden planting

From reinvigorating tired borders to a complete re-design, I create unique outdoor spaces tailored to each client’s needs. I personally choose the best, peak condition plants for each situation, sourcing from reputable specialists.

Garden maintenance

Whatever the size or condition of your garden, I can bring it back to its best with careful pruning, planting to fill gaps, weeding, mulching and lawn maintenance. I leave your garden tidy, clear of debris and ready for you to enjoy.

Recent garden projects

About me

I'm a plants specialist who works collaboratively with clients to create lasting and beautiful gardens. A former magazine, newspaper and digital journalist, I trained at the Chelsea Physic Garden. I work with a trusted team of hard landscapers to make gardens designed for you to relax in and enjoy year-round.
