Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered

For over 100 years, we’ve been the trusted voice for plumbing and heating in Leeds. Here is everything you need to know about our services. If you have any more questions, do not hesitate to get in touch.
Contact Us Today
Do you offer emergency services outside of normal business hours?

Yes. While our standard office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 17:00 PM, we provide a comprehensive 24/7 emergency call-out service. Our team is available from 8:00 AM to 17:00 PM on weekdays and operates round-the-clock during weekends to handle urgent issues like burst pipes or gas emergencies.

What areas do you serve?
We are based in Leeds and provide trusted plumbing, heating, and gas services to homeowners, landlords, and businesses across Leeds, Yorkshire, and the surrounding areas.
Are your engineers qualified to work on gas appliances?
Absolutely. All of our engineers are Gas Safe registered and undergo regular audits to ensure they maintain best practices. We handle everything from gas leak detection and pipework installation to boiler servicing and landlord safety checks.
Do you provide services for commercial properties?
Yes. We offer a wide range of plumbing and heating solutions catered to both residential and commercial clients. Our team is experienced in handling large-scale projects as well as smaller domestic repairs, including affordable service bundles for business clients.
What specific services do you offer for bathrooms and heating?
We provide a start-to-finish service for bathrooms, including design consultation, product specification, and full installation. For heating, we specialize in high-efficiency boilers, combi boiler repairs, powerflushing, and modern renewable heating solutions and smart control systems.
