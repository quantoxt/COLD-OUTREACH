About Us

For over 60 years, Marcus has been the trusted locksmith partner in our community.

Founded locally in 1963, we remain an independent, locally-owned and operated business dedicated to excellence. With decades of experience across automotive, residential and commercial sectors, our fully trained and certified locksmiths bring both the skill and the care you deserve.

We believe that every lock and key tells a story of trust. That’s why we handle every job – big or small – with precision, care, and honesty. From emergency lockouts to advanced access control systems, our team uses modern tools and techniques to ensure your complete peace of mind.

We’re not just locksmiths — we’re your security partners, dedicated to protecting what matters most.
