Award Winning!***** Face Painter & Balloon Artist, & balloon decoration Birmingham, West Midlands, UK based. 

Sara’s Parlour Face Painting is a contemporary face and body art company based in Birmingham, West Midlands UK. We offer both design & entertainment services. Catering  for both children and adults. For all events in Birmingham, West Midlands, & UK wide, we can even be booked to work abroad! (Please see our client list). Established in 2007 (We’re celebrating 19 years this year!). 

We’re celebrating! Winner of the ‘Amazing Artist Award’ at the 2023 Dyslexia Awards!! & 20 Years as a professional face & body artist & company in 2024!!

Sara and her talented team provide beautiful designs and walking works of art for every type of client and event. From small private parties to large scale/multi location corporate bookings – In short anything involving face painting, body art & physical transformation!

Alongside face and body art Sara’s Parlour offers a range of other services, including: Balloon modelling, Balloon decoration, Children’s entertainment professionals, art workshops, mural art & window art painting & more!

Sara is a trained artist and also teaches face paint skills one to one for adults, and for children’s workshops (for both parties and learning).

Sara can be booked as a sole artist, a duo, or a larger team – handpicking just the right artist/s for you. Whether it’s pretty and fun children’s face painting for a birthday party, or sophisticated and exciting adult looks for special occasions (such as birthdays, weddings and Hen parties/Do’s). All artists are highly creative, artist trained, reliable, and PLI Insured. DBS (CRB) checked artists are also available. We offer artists working with face painting and body paint, glitter, jewel adornments, temporary and glitter tattoos, henna, neon/UV painting, hair feathers, Special effects, airbrush…

Please take a look around our website to find out more, see our amazing pictures – and please don’t hesitate to contact us!
