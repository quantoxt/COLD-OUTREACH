WELCOME TO FD ELETRICAL SOLUTIONS – NICEIC Approved Electrical Contractors

FD Electrical Solutions is an electrical company based in the Northfield area. We operate throughout Birmingham and the surrounding areas. We offer electrical services to the Domestic and Commercial sectors as well as Private Landlords from a simply changing a socket  to complete installation of a new system and maintenance of your existing one. Our team of experienced professional contractors are City & Guilds qualified and Part P registered. Quality of work and Customer Satisfaction is of upmost importance to us and we will strive to achieve this. We are covered by our £2 Million Public Liability Insurance and all work is covered by the NICEIC platinum promise.
