When Design, Quality & Experience Are Important to you.

LOXLEYS KITCHENS & BATHROOMS of SHEFFIELD: to dedicate our time to you we have moved to an appointment system for our showroom.

Shifting to an appointment-only model brings a whole new level of service and refinement. Here’s how it benefits both you and our design team:

 Personalised Attention

Every consultation becomes a focused, one-on-one session. No distractions, no interruptions—just dedicated time to explore your ideas and preferences.

 Thoughtful Design Process

With fewer time constraints, we can dive deeper into your project: discuss layout options, materials, and finishes without rushing decisions.

 Exclusive Studio Access

You’ll have our Sheffield studio all to yourself during your visit—perfect for viewing samples and experiencing the space without any crowd or noise.

 Flexible & Efficient Scheduling

Appointments streamline our workflow. We prepare for your visit in advance, ensuring everything you want to see or discuss is ready and waiting.

 Confidence & Clarity

Knowing your design team is fully focused on you builds trust and helps turn ideas into actionable plans—right there in the consultation.

It’s a quieter, more curated experience—ideal for projects where quality and detail matter. If you’re thinking of booking a visit, I can help you shape a prep checklist or suggest questions worth asking.
