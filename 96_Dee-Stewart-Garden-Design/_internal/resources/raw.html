A beautiful garden can add so much to your life – an outdoor space to share with family and friends, a stylish entertaining area or a peaceful sanctuary - or perhaps an addictive pastime! Whether you have a small courtyard or a country estate, I believe that a well-designed garden should form an extension of your home. I work with my clients to capture their needs and aspirations, and translate them into reality, creating gardens to suit their lifestyle and their environment. Based in the leafy suburbs of Liverpool, I am a garden designer offering a comprehensive range of garden services across the north-west, which I tailor to your requirements with complete flexibility – you may want a full landscape design, a garden makeover, colourful planting schemes or perhaps help with a tricky area. Or maybe you are looking for a wildlife-friendly garden, somewhere to grow fruit and veg, a green wall, a water feature...the options are endless. Whether you’re considering a modernist contemporary look or prefer a romantic classical feel, from Mediterranean or prairie, cottage or jungle, I’d love to help you create your perfect garden! Contact: email - dee@deestewartgardens.co.uk tel - 07974 213354 Liverpool-based and throughout Merseyside, Wirral, Manchester, Lancashire & Cheshire, and into N Wales.
