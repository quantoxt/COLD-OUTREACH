Manchester Plasterers P. Taylor & Sons undertake all disciplines of internal plastering, external plastering and spray rendering. Our services range from cracked walls and ceilings to renovations and new builds. We specialise in K Rend and Parex Spray Rendering Services.

All of our staff are PASMA & CSCS qualified with over 30 years combined experience.

We pay particular attention to the fine details of our clients' requests, resulting in quality well priced work and satisfied customers. All our plastering work is guaranteed and we are fully insured. We have a UTR number, follow strict health & safety guidelines and provide risk assessments.

As experienced Manchester plasterers, we pride ourselves on the quality of the plastering and rendering work we undertake and guarantee the highest standards to both domestic and commercial customers. Based in Manchester, P Taylor and Sons Plastering Ltd. are ideally located to serve surrounding areas including the Midlands, Northern England and North Wales. Our quotes for plastering and rendering work are always carefully calculated allowing us to be competetive with our quotes without the any impact on the quality of our plastering work.

Our mission is to ensure that our customers are always happy and satisfied with the plastering services we provide. P Taylor & Sons means quality plastering services but don't take our word for it feel free to ask our customers.
