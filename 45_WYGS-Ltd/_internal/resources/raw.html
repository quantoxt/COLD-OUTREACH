Available Grants

  • Boiler Upgrade Scheme

    The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) is a government initiative in England and Wales designed to support the transition to low-carbon heating systems.

    Read more

  • The Warm Homes: Local Grant (WHLG)

    The Warm Homes: Local Grant (WHLG) is a government initiative in England aimed at improving the energy efficiency of homes that are not connected to the mains gas grid.

     

    Read more

  • Warm Homes: Social Housing Fund Wave 3

    Warm Homes: Social Housing Fund Wave 3 supports landlords to upgrade inefficient homes with insulation, low‑carbon heating and energy‑saving measures, improving comfort and cutting bills for residents.

     

    Read more

  • The Warm Homes Plan

    Labour’s Warm Homes Plan proposes upgrading millions of homes with insulation, heat pumps and energy‑saving measures, cutting bills and improving comfort while accelerating the UK’s shift to clean heat. Due to be launched late 2026.

About Us

Founded in 2014 by Glenn and Dave, WYGS Ltd has grown from a grant‑delivery specialist into one of the region’s leading experts in solar panels, battery storage, air‑source heat pumps and EV charging solutions. With decades of industry experience, the team now delivers cutting‑edge renewable technologies that help homeowners reduce bills, lower carbon emissions and upgrade their homes for the future.
