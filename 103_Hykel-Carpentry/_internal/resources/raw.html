About Alex

Hykel Carpentry

Hykel Carpentry offers high-quality interior woodwork, joinery, and custom cabinets, handmade by a qualified carpenter with 10 years of experience in Bristol homes. Operating independently from a well-equipped home workshop, I focus on  the right-sized projects, ensuring good value for cost-conscious clients. I am deeply passionate about my craft and the materials I work with, and help you choose the best sustainable, quality options. Thanks to strong relationships with local Bristol suppliers, I source everything from locally grown hardwoods and reclaimed timber to Birch Ply and Valchromat. Most importantly, I treat your home with absolute respect. Let’s have a genuine conversation about your next project. Please feel free to get in touch.
