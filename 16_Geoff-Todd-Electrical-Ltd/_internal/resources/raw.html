COVID-19

The health, safety and wellbeing of our employees and clients is of the upmost importance to us. We have put measures and procedures in place to protect everyone concerned with our business during these unprecedented times.

These measures are in accordance with the HSE and the Government's 5 step plan:
"Working Safely during coronavirus (COVID-19)"

Covid-19 Risk Assessment

We have carried out a risk assessment in line with the HSE guidance and shared this with our employees. Site specific Risk Assesments will be provided to our clients prior to commencing works.

Handwashing & hygiene

Employees will have access to handwashing facilities on most sites and where this is not possible, they have been provided with hand saniser. Surface cleaning materials have also been provided.

Working from home

Our office based staff have been provided with the means to work from home and have remote access to the company's systems. Meetings can be carried out using software such as Zoom, Skype & Teams.

2m Social Distancing

Wherever possible, 2m distancing will be maintained between people.
Signage and markings will be used where necessary and one way system used. Operatives wil travel individually and have staggered breaks to assist with distancing.

Managing transmission risk

Where 2m distancing cannot be achieved, other measures such as minimising the activity time, back-to-back or side-to-side working and the use of screens or barriers will be employed. Any teams of opertives will be fixed to reduce contact.
