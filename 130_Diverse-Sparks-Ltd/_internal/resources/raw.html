Diverse Sparks Ltd

‘Small enough to care, large enough to look after your needs’

We are an approved, registered, electrical contracting firm which is based in Castle Bromwich, Birmingham.

We cover all areas of the west midlands and also complete projects in London.

We strive to achieve outstanding customer service and it is our main priority to ensure our customers will continue to use our services. We deliver exactly what we promise which is friendly, on time, clean and reliable service at a competitive price.

We provide services such as,

- Full rewires
- Consumer unit upgrades
- Landlords certificates
- Security alarms
- CCTV
- Network cabling ( cat 5 or 6 )
- Fire Alarm ( design and installation )
- 3 phase supplies
- Emergency lighting ( design and installation )
- All fault finding
- Shop displays

WEE REGULATIONS
Improving our carbon footprint is important to us, we are learning every day about the steps we can take to become a more sustainable company, shifting our focus to renewable resources and materials. To support this, all of our waste is disposed of correctly under the WEEE regulations.

In addition to the services listed above, we are also able to provide free, no obligation quotes and expert advice. All of the work we carry out is guaranteed and we are covered by public liability insurance.
