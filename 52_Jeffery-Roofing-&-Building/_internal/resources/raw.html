L Jeffery Roofing for North London, Enfield, Haringey, Crouch End

L Jeffery Roofing is a family run business based in the heart of North London servicing Haringey, Enfield and Crouch End.

We specialise in new roofs, roof repairs, flat roofing, pointing of chimney stacks, and Velux window installation. In fact we cover all types of roofing work.

We also provide an emergency call out service, so whether it’s a leak, broken tile, or missing slate, just give us a call.

We’re very proud of the work we do, and are equally proud of the way we do it. Our service is so good our customers stay loyal and recommend us to others.

Please browse though our website to view photos of the work we have undertaken and to see testimonial’s from some of our satisfied customers.

All of our roofers are fully insured, insurance documents are available upon request.

Please contact us for a free quotation and advice on any roofing issue and we will be more than happy to help.

Our Services

Years of Expertise

Chimneys

It is important that chimneys are looked after as they can deteriorate or collapse if they are not maintained. We offer chimney maintenance and repair services including chimney repairs including re-pointing and re-bedding of chimney pots, installation of lead soakers and flashings and more!

New Roofs

We specialise in new roofs and our experienced and professional roofers can install new roofs to the highest-quality. Often when roof tiles keep slipping, falling down and breaking you may require a new roof. We fit all kinds of new roofs from slate roofs, tiled roofs, flat roofs, shed roofs, garage roofs and solar roofs.

Emergency Call Outs

Although we are based in the London Borough of Enfield we will come out to any roofing emergency within the M25. We can usually be with you within the hour. If you live in Crouch End, Muswell Hill, Islington, Finsbury Park, Stoke Newington or nearby areas we can usually get to you within 20 minutes.
