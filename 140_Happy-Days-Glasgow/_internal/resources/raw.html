Meet Jo

Our creative activities team is lead by principal artist Jo McMahon

Jo McMahon

Hi, I’m Jo, the company director at Happy Days Glasgow. I have always had a passion for the arts and people; I studied drawing and painting at Glasgow School of Art and later got a degree in Community Arts which has taken me all across the city working in a number of professional and community settings.

I love working with children and in recent years have lead community projects working with toddler groups, additional needs art sessions, after school art projects and children’s theatre.

Face painting and creative activities at events gives me a quick opportunity to connect and create a beautiful design and I love to see those happy faces in the mirror at the end.
