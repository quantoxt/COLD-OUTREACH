About Us

Bark and Gardens is an independent Bristol gardening company established by Jono Startup who is an RHS Qualified gardener. We provide garden maintenance, tree surgery and soft landscaping services to a wide range of commercial and residential properties & clients. Furthermore, we are also able to clear and remove any garden waste once the job is done as we are Bristol trade composting waste licensed.

With over 10 years’ experience Jono and his team provide services to gardens across Bristol and the surrounding areas. In addition to being RHS Qualified, Jono is also NPTC (National Proficiency Test’s Council) Level 3 in Forestry & Arboriculture (Tree care) enabling him to confidently provide tree cutting and pruning services.

Available for single or regular on-going garden maintenance visits. specialising in the pruning of ornamental and fruiting trees, shrubs, border maintenance, planting, mulching and hedge cutting.
