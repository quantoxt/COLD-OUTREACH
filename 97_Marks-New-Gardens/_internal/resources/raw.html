Welcome to Mark’s New Gardens!

We first opened for business in 1995 and have been providing garden maintenance services in Liverpool and surrounding areas ever since.

Mark’s New Gardens works mainly in domestic and commercial work as a garden & grounds maintenance business. We usually cut an overgrown area down then maintain it and turn it into a garden through the contract and any work in between.

We offer a full range of services, ranging from small local projects to major commercial work and everything in between, such as contract work for Schools, housing associations, hotels, nursing homes, property management companies, landlords and many small gardens.

We can offer you a no obligation quote now, so contact us for more information.

We have professionally trained staff who can help you with a wide range of gardening services. The experts at Mark’s New Gardens can handle your requirements, and they have the experience and competence to offer you assistance. We know how much you love your garden, and that’s why we put extra effort into our work. We strive for excellence in our gardening practices and techniques. Please don’t hesitate to contact Mark’s New Gardens for more information!
