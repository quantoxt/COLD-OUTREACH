Three generations of Heywoods

Les Heywood & Son Ltd is located in the picturesque town of Holmfirth, just outside Huddersfield, where ‘Last of the Summer Wine’ was filmed. Started in 1966 by Mr Les Heywood and now run by Alan Heywood, our engineers pride themselves on quality, cleanliness and punctuality — with over 150 years of experience between them and 4,000 clients on our books.
