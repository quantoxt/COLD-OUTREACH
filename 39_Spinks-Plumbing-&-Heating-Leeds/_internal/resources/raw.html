We’re Spinks, a family business established in 1926

AE Spink Ltd, known as Spinks, was founded in 1926 in Doncaster. The business began as an ironmongers and plumbers’ merchant, trading under the name Stavely & Spink. As a family-run company, it quickly earned a strong reputation for its extensive stock range and knowledgeable staff, attracting repeat custom from both tradespeople and the general public. This success drove steady expansion, and today Spinks operates four Plumbing and Heating branches, two Building Supplies and Brick Centres, three Bathroom Showrooms, and a dedicated Renewables Centre.
