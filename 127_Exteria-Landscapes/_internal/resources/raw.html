About Us

Jonathan Crossan - Bearsden & Milngavie based Landscape gardener and decking specialist 

We create contemporary gardens, courtyards and outdoor spaces in Bearsden, Milngavie and the North Glasgow area. Projects include city, courtyard, and family gardens, licensed premises and disabled access solutions for local authorities across Scotland.
 
In town, the emphasis has always been on low maintenance and this has evolved into an important element of all our gardens. 
 
Jonathan Crossan studied Horticulture and Landscape Management at the Scottish Agricultural college and Glasgow University and graduated with a BSC in 2005, and he has designed and created over 100 projects.
 
All work and projects are covered via comprehensive insurance, so peace of mind is ensured.
