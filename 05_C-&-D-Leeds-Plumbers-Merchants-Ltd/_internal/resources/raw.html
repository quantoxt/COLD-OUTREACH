Plumbing & Heating Specialists

C&D is a Leeds based independent plumbing merchant, offering a full range of plumbing and heating products to all sectors of the industry. We provide services and products to both trade and the general public ex-stock from our Leeds based depot, backed up by reliable national delivery facilities to ensure we meet your supply deadlines.

Our goal is to exceed our customers' expectations at all levels. Our experienced staff are available to answer your questions and to source the products to meet your needs – just call us, email us via the contact page on this website or drop in for a coffee and a chat! Over the years, we have encountered every type of plumbing problem and always come up with a solution.

Opening hours are:
Monday - Friday 7.00am - 3.00pm
Saturday & Sunday Closed
Nationwide delivery service available Monday - Friday.
