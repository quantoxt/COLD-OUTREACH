ABOUT US

WELCOME Based in Glasgow, S Fraser Joiners & Preservation specialise in Rising Damp, Wet Rot, Dry Rot, Condensation Control, Woodworm Eradication and Basement Waterproofing. For over 10 years, S Fraser Joiners & Preservation have been providing preservation and general joinery services to both domestic and commercial customers. We have a growing reputation as the contractor of choice for householders, Landlords, Property Management and Letting Agencies for a wide range of joinery and preservation work. S Fraser Joiners & Preservation cover Glasgow, Strathclyde, Lanarkshire, Renfrewshire and Central Scotland.
