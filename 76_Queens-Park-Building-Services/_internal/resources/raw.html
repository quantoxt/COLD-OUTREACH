About Us

Bespoke Constructions

Queen’s Park Building Services is a family owned business established in Glasgow in 1989 by John and Jackie Russell. It has steadily expanded, building up a secure client base, with a large core of long standing tradesmen employed.

We pride our self  in giving clients a personal service and value for money. We undertake both small and large projects for private, commercial and local authorities, mainly throughout Glasgow and the West of Scotland. This can range from new buildings to loft conversions, property extensions and refurbishments, all to a high quality finish. We can call on all trades and specialist services.
