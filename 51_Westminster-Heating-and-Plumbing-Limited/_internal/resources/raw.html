About Us

Westminster is a growing family business, set up in 2000 by Richard Sears. We provide trustworthy, friendly, professional engineers who execute your jobs to the highest standards. We run an ongoing training programme that ensures that all of our team are certified, qualified, insured and fully compliant with regulations.

We are proud that our business has developed organically through recommendation and word of mouth and we have worked for some very prestigious and exciting clients. Our reputation is key, we always deliver above and beyond.
