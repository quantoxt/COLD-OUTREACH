NFRC Publications

NFRC produces a wide rage of technical publications covering numerous roofing disciplines. Some documents are free to NFRC Members or require purchasing through the shop, whilst others are freely available for the wider industry and general public.

Find out more

In addition to NFRC as a membership trade association, there are a number of complimentary schemes and services available through the broader NFRC group. 

NFRC CPS

Part of the wider NFRC Group, NFRC Competent Person Scheme (CPS) allows registered roofers to self-certify that their roof refurbishment work meets the latest building regulations, saving the contractor both time and money.

NFRC CPS

Roofing Careers Service

This service provides support to employer members and their current and future employees through the entire careers cycle, offering information for parents, career starters and those embarking on a roofing career.

Roofing Careers Service

UK Roofing Awards

An industry-wide event that recognises and rewards outstanding standards of workmanship and safety within the UK roofing industry, all wrapped up in a glittering annual awards ceremony.

UK Roofing Awards

RoofCERT

The UK’s only national roofing accreditation scheme providing individuals with recognition and proof of their skill set, whilst helping to professionalise the industry and prove their competence to employers and clients.

RoofCERT

NFRC Heritage Register

Only roofing companies with suitably qualified operatives are listed on the National Heritage Roofing Contractors Register. They promote an understanding and training of heritage roofs, whilst giving assurance to the property owners.

NFRC Heritage Register
