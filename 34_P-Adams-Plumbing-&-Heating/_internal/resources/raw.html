Boiler Repair & New Boiler Installation

We'd rather repair your boiler than replace it - when it makes sense. Sometimes a small part gets you years more service. Other times the repair cost stacks up against an ageing unit and a new boiler is the better call. Peter will tell you which is which.

Need a Boiler Repair?

No heat, no hot water, or a fault code on the display? Call Peter direct – he’ll talk through what’s wrong, give you guidance on what’s likely involved, and book a visit. For boiler emergencies, he’ll usually get to you the same day.

  • Same-day callouts for boiler breakdowns

  • Honest repair-or-replace advice

Choosing a New Boiler

If your boiler is past its best or the repair cost doesn’t add up, we’ll talk you through a replacement that fits your home and budget. Peter installs across all major brands and is accredited by Ideal and Vaillant, with manufacturer-backed warranties up to 10 years on selected models.

  • All major boiler brands supplied and fitted

  • Ideal and Vaillant accredited installer

  • One-day install for most replacements
